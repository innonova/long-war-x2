# The Officer Training School as a player uses it (a new campaign at EW's base: tools/test/new-campaign.sh 1 --begin):
# setup places the school; then by clicks Barracks → OFFICER TRAINING SCHOOL → Squad Size I → PURCHASE → EW's CONFIRM
# UPGRADE → YES, the squad one larger in Long War's state and the row marked bought.
lw base set 6 2 12
lw give Supplies 200
# (The school's first squad size needs 40 ranks in the barracks: a release campaign's soldiers are rookies, so eight of
# them are promoted five times; development builds promote the rookies already, PromoteRookies.)
lw rankup 0 5
lw rankup 1 5
lw rankup 2 5
lw rankup 3 5
lw rankup 4 5
lw rankup 5 5
lw rankup 6 5
lw rankup 7 5
lwsave squad ots|squad size [0-9]+
tab BARRACKS
expect UILWEWHub
click OFFICER TRAINING SCHOOL
expect OTS
see TOTAL RANKS
click Squad Size I
click PURCHASE
expect DialogueBox
see CONFIRM UPGRADE
click =YES
expect OTS
lwdiff squad ots|squad size [0-9]+|1
see Bought.
shot bought
back
expect UILWEWHub
