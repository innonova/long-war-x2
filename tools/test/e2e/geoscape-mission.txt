# A mission from the Geoscape as a player takes it (a campaign at EW's base: tools/test/new-campaign.sh 1 --begin): time run
# until abductions are reported (setup; other decisions left waiting), then by clicks:
# Mission Control's alert (abductions: EW's site selection), XCOM 2's squad select in EW's hangar, LAUNCH, the briefing,
# a soldier moved (XCOM 2's right click on a tile), the turn ended (Backspace), the aliens' turn, XCOM's second turn,
# Restart Mission from the pause menu, the aliens there again.
lw arm now
until decisions|abductions in|run 720|12
click MISSION CONTROL
expect Alert
# (A UFO seen or landed meanwhile comes first: let go, as a player might.)
maybe IGNORE CONTACT
maybe =IGNORE
maybe IGNORE CONTACT
maybe =IGNORE
shot alert
# (The abductions' alert, or, once another alert was let go, their entry in Mission Control's list: EW's list opens the
# site selection itself.)
maybe VIEW ABDUCTION SITE
maybe ALIEN ABDUCTIONS
see ABDUCTION SITE
click Confirm
expect SquadSelect
shot squad-select
click LAUNCH
waitfor DropShipBriefing 240
wait 25
# (EW's situation for the mission in the briefing.)
see abduction in progress
key Space
waitfor ready=1 180
shot mission-start
rmb
wait 6
# (A move spent one action point, or both on a far tile: a dash.)
lwsee units|~ ap [01] sight
key Back
# (XCOM's second turn: the state line's round, the turns of the side acting now.)
waitfor round=2 240
waitfor turn=eTeam_XCom busy=0 240
shot second-turn
lwsee enemies|team
# Restart Mission from the pause menu (in every mission's, the port's): the mission again, Long War's aliens in it (they
# had been lost on a restart, 2026-11-23).
esc
expect PauseMenu
click Restart Mission
expect DialogueBox
key Enter
# (The map loaded again: its first round; the drop-ship briefing comes and goes faster than the state's poll at times.)
waitfor round=1 240
wait 25
key Space
waitfor ready=1 180
wait 5
lwsee enemies|team
shot restarted
