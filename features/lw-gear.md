## Purpose

Research is only half of Long War's strategy layer: what it unlocks is
built in engineering and carried by the soldiers. Long War's arsenal is
its own: eleven weapon families in five tiers, which classes may carry
which, armour, and the costs to build them. This feature brings that
gear, step 2 of `port-plan.md`, then engineering builds it.

## What the person should see

- Long War's soldiers carry Long War's weapons, with Long War's names
  and numbers (damage, crit, aim, shots), each class only what Long War
  lets it carry.
- New Long War soldiers start with Long War's kit:
  - the assault rifle;
  - the shotgun for Assaults;
  - the sniper rifle for Snipers;
  - the SAW for Gunners;
  - a pistol, except for Rocketeers and Gunners.
- The weapons look like XCOM 2's, Long War's lasers and plasma like the
  originals where War of the Chosen has them (its Legacy weapons).
- Then armour, and engineering: research unlocks items, built for Long
  War's money, alloys, elerium and engineers.

## Requirements

- **Long War's numbers, generated** from the player's `DefaultGameCore.ini`
  (`Weapons=`, `Armors=`, `ItemBalance_Normal`), as the classes are.
- **Its names come from the player's Long War install**, written by setup,
  never committed.
- **Long War's rules for who carries what**
  (`XGStorage.IsClassEquippable`, `GetInfinitePrimary`, the classes'
  `eWeaponType`), made XCOM 2's weapon categories.
- **The phases:**
  1. Weapons for the classes, and the starting kit.
  2. Armour.
  3. Engineering: building what research unlocks.
  4. The Foundry's projects.

## Facts (read from Long War's code and ini, 2026-11-01)

- **Families by tier** (ballistic, laser, gauss, pulse, plasma: Long War's
  item numbers):

  | Family | Items |
  |---|---|
  | pistol | 2, 8, 13 |
  | machine pistol | 236–240 |
  | SMG | 231–235 |
  | carbine | 32, 251, 53, 252, 14 |
  | rifle | 212, 215, 3, 9, 15 |
  | battle rifle | 241–245 |
  | marksman's rifle | 226–230 |
  | sniper rifle | 213, 216, 6, 12, 18 |
  | shotgun | 4, 10, 16, 1, 99 |
  | autorifle | 214, 217, 5, 11, 17 |
  | LMG | 246–250 |
  | sawed-off shotgun | 254 |
  | arc rifle | 253 |

  Long War reuses EXALT's item numbers for its ballistic and laser basics,
  and renamed Enemy Within's (`eItem_AssaultRifle` is its Gauss Rifle).
- **The classes' weapon property** (`XGStrategySoldier`, `eWeaponType`):

  | Class | Property |
  |---|---|
  | Sniper | Sniper |
  | Scout | Overheats |
  | Rocketeer | Heavy |
  | Gunner | Support |
  | Medic, Infantry | Rifle |
  | Engineer | UnlimitedAmmo |
  | Assault | Assault |

- **Who may carry what** (`XGStorage.IsClassEquippable`):
  - an Any-class weapon fits everyone;
  - Assault, Scout and Engineer also take Rifle weapons;
  - a Pistol weapon fits everyone but Rocketeers and Gunners, and the
    sawed-off only Medics, Infantry, Assaults, Scouts and Engineers;
  - otherwise a weapon fits a class whose property it has.
- **The starting kit, unlimited** (`GetInfinitePrimary`): Gunner the SAW,
  Assault the shotgun, Sniper the sniper rifle, the rest the assault
  rifle; Rocketeers also a rocket launcher (`GetInfiniteSecondary`).
- **Packed fields:**
  - `iSuppression`: shots between reloads (low), with Ammo Conservation
    (high);
  - `ItemBalance_Normal` `iElerium`: elerium (low) and meld (high);
  - `iAlloys`: alloys (low) and a structure requirement (high).

## Report (2026-11-01, phase 1: weapons and the starting kit)

**Long War's 55 soldier weapons are XCOM 2's** (`X2Item_LWWeapons`,
config `XComLWGear.ini`, generated by `scripts/gear-config.mjs` with the
rules in `scripts/lw-gear.mjs`).
- Long War's numbers:
  - damage, with XCOM 2's spread of 1 and a critical hit half again;
  - crit chance and aim;
  - shots between reloads (pistols unlimited, as XCOM 2's);
  - damage to cover.
- XCOM 2's shots by kind: rifles, sniper rifles (move or shoot), pistols.
- No upgrade slots, since Long War has none.
- **Who may carry it** is its XCOM 2 category, named after the classes
  Long War lets carry it (`lw_sniper_scout`, …). The classes' allowed
  weapons are generated from the same rules, so they cannot drift.
- **Its look** is an XCOM 2 weapon's (model, attachments, images,
  sounds), copied once all templates are built.
- **Its name, plural and summary are Long War's** (`makeWeaponWords`),
  and its tactical info is Long War's summary, else a line from its
  numbers.

**The starting kit is Long War's.**
- Each class's squaddie loadout is its unlimited starting weapons: the
  assault rifle, Snipers the sniper rifle, Gunners the SAW, Assaults the
  shotgun, and a pistol except for Rocketeers and Gunners.
- **Found on the way:** XCOM 2's item manager reads its loadouts from
  `GameData`, so the loadouts the class config had carried in
  `XComClassData.ini` were silently ignored all along (unnoticed while
  they named XCOM 2's rifles the rookies already had). They are now in
  `XComGameData.ini`.
- Soldiers promoted before this build, or by any path that keeps a
  weapon their class may not carry, are re-armed (`LWX2Gear.ArmAll`)
  whenever the armory, squad selection, the after-action report or the
  Geoscape opens. The old weapon goes back to the stores.

**Checked in the game** (new campaign, control port `arm`, armory):
- every Long War soldier carries their kit, and Rocketeers and Gunners
  have no pistol;
- all 55 weapons took their looks;
- the Sniper holds the Legacy ballistic sniper rifle (the original's
  look);
- the loadout shows "Assault Rifle, damage 3–5, 3 shots, 8 %";
- the SAW is marked "GUNNER ONLY", and XCOM 2's rifle "unavailable to
  Sniper".

### Decisions

- **The looks (a taste decision, for the person).** Where War of the
  Chosen has a Legacy (TLE) weapon of the type, the ballistic, laser and
  plasma tiers take it: those are the original's guns. Elsewhere, and for
  gauss and pulse, XCOM 2's conventional, magnetic and beam models. Also:
  - the Vektor rifle for marksman's rifles;
  - the Templar's autopistol for machine pistols;
  - the assault rifle's model for SMGs, carbines and battle rifles, so
    those three look alike for now.
- **One XCOM 2 category per set of classes allowed**, since XCOM 2
  allows weapons by category and Long War by property, and a Long War
  weapon has several properties.
- **Damage spread 1, critical +50 %**: XCOM 2's way of rolling, sized
  to Long War's damage.

### Left open

- **XCOM 2's labels:** it marks a weapon a class may not carry "<class>
  ONLY" with the first class allowed: the shotgun reads "SCOUT ONLY",
  though Medics, Engineers, Assaults and Infantry carry it too.
- XCOM 2's own starting weapons are still in the stores and lists. Long
  War's classes cannot carry them, but XCOM 2's rookies still use them.
- War of the Chosen's Skirmish Mode still arms Long War's classes with
  XCOM 2's weapons; to check, and to move to Long War's.
- **Armour** (phase 2), and **building** what research unlocks (phase
  3): every weapon but the starting kit exists but cannot be built yet.
- Rockets, grenades and other small items.

## Report (2026-11-01, phase 3: engineering)

**Engineering builds what research unlocks, by Long War's rules**
(`LWEngineering`, the projects in the Geoscape's state, an ENGINEERING
screen from the Geoscape). Its data:
- costs, days and engineers are `ItemBalance_Normal`, generated into
  `XComLWGear.ini` (`+Builds`);
- what each item needs first is read from Long War's
  `XGItemTree.BuildItems` into the Tree's `data/itemtree.json`
  (`scripts/upk_itemtree.py`, numbers only).

Its rules, read from Long War's code:
- **An item can be built** once its tech is researched and its Foundry
  project done.
- **The cost is paid when the project starts:** money, alloys, elerium and
  meld, plus one of the item it needs per item built (the alien weapon for
  plasma, the shotgun for the sawed-off).
- **The engineers set the pace, they are not a gate.** Long War took out
  the original's "Not Enough Engineers!" (diffed against Enemy Within).
  - A project of n items has n × the item's engineers on it, however many
    XCOM has.
  - Its work is days × engineers × 24 hours, done at Long War's
    `GetWorkPerHour`: about all of XCOM's engineers an hour when the
    project has that many, and less and less the further it is short of
    them.
  - The whole batch is done together.
  - So at the start (10 engineers): a laser pistol takes 15 days, a laser
    rifle 18, two laser rifles 26, a gauss carbine 105.
- **A finished project stops the clock**, as a ready satellite does, and
  its items go to the stores (shown under STORES, with the projects).

**Checked in the game** (new campaign, control port):
- the sawed-off was built in a day;
- `GiveTech LWTech_LaserWeapons` unlocked the seven laser weapons, short
  of alloys;
- with 50 alloys given, two laser rifles were begun (26 days, 30
  engineers on them) and arrived on day 27, two in the stores;
- Alloy Cannon unlocked the gauss carbine at 105 days.

New control port commands:
- `builds`;
- `build <item> [n]`;
- `give <template> <n>`, a test aid.

### Decisions

- **Long War's timed projects, not XCOM 2's instant building.** XCOM 2's
  own Build Items screen still lists none of Long War's gear (it cannot be
  built there). Engineering is a screen of the Long War Geoscape, whose
  clock it runs on.
- **No rush** (Long War's "rush" at 1.5× the cost) and no workshops yet:
  the cash cost already takes the workshop cut (`ScaleCost`), with none
  built.
- **Items that wait on a Foundry project** (the arc thrower rifle) stay
  locked until the Foundry is ported.

### Left open

- Alien weapons are not looted yet (captives and stun), so plasma, which
  consumes one, cannot be built.
- Armour (phase 2) and its builds; the Foundry (phase 4); workshops (the
  base).
- Long War's weapon perks while carried (`ItemBalance_Normal`'s high
  alloy digits are a perk, not a cost: `XGStorage` grants it) and
  `BalanceMods` (mobility, will by weapon).

## Report (2026-11-01, phases 2 and 4: armour and the Foundry)

**Long War's 14 soldier armours are XCOM 2's** (`X2Item_LWArmors`,
`X2Ability_LWArmorStats`, generated with the weapons into `XComLWGear.ini`):
- **Long War's numbers** (HP, defence, will, mobility) are a stat ability
  of the armour, shown in the armory's stat lines.
- Fire and poison immunity (Titan, Archangel) is an immunity effect;
  grapple (Kestrel, Banshee, Corsair, Shadow) is XCOM 2's `Grapple`.
- Two small items, which every Long War armour has, are XCOM 2's second
  utility slot.
- **Tac Vest and Tac Armor are unlimited**, as Long War never builds them.
  Long War soldiers wear Tac Armor: one in XCOM 2's Kevlar is changed
  when the soldiers are armed (`LWX2Gear`), as the weapons are.
- **The rest are built in engineering**, at Long War's costs and with its
  requirements. Archangel consumes a Titan Armor, as Long War's
  "hardcoded to require Titan Suit".
- **The looks:**
  - each armour takes an XCOM 2 armour's images and sounds;
  - XCOM 2 picks a soldier's torso, arms, legs and helmet by the armour's
    template name, so that armour's body parts are cloned for each Long
    War armour (884 parts).
  - Checked in a mission: the squad in Tac Armor renders whole, with the
    HP bars including the armour's.
- **Names:** Long War's (`makeArmourWords`).

**The Foundry** (`LWEngineering`, the ENGINEERING screen's FOUNDRY view,
control port `foundry [id]`). Its 46 projects come from `FoundryBalance`,
with what each needs first from the Tree's `data/techtree.json`. Long
War's rules:
- **To start a project:**
  - its tech must be researched;
  - its item must be in stores.
- **The cost is paid at the start:** money, alloys, elerium, fragments
  and meld (the high digits of `iFragments`), and the item, `iNumItems`
  of it.
- **The time:**
  - its work is days × engineers × 24 hours;
  - each research credit held that covers it takes its share off, at
    least a day each;
  - the work is done at the same `GetWorkPerHour` as items.
- **A finished project** stops the clock, is remembered, and unlocks what
  waits on it.
  - Checked: New Combat Systems (60 days at 10 engineers) unlocked the
    Arc Rifle.
- **Names and summaries:** Long War's (`makeFoundryWords`), carried by
  never-researchable tech templates (`LWFoundry_<id>`).

### Decisions

- **The armours' looks (a taste decision):**
  - Tac Vest and Tac Armor look like Kevlar;
  - Phalanx and Carapace like medium plated, Kestrel and Banshee like
    light plated (Spider), Aegis like heavy plated (EXO);
  - Corsair, Seraph and Shadow like light powered (Wraith), Titan like
    heavy powered (W.A.R.);
  - Archangel, Aurora and Vortex like medium powered (Warden).
- **Long War soldiers wear Tac Armor**, Enemy Within's Kevlar, rather
  than the Tac Vest.
- **No heavy weapon slot** from XCOM 2's heavy armours: a Long War
  Rocketeer's launcher is its own matter.
- **One Foundry project at a time.** Long War allows one per Foundry
  built, and the base where Foundries are built is not ported yet. XCOM
  counts as having one. *(Superseded the same day by `lw-base.md`: one
  project per Foundry built in the base, none at the start.)*
- **A research credit gained cuts a Foundry project's time only if it
  starts later.** Long War also cuts the one under way.

### Left open

- **What Foundry projects do:**
  - Each project is remembered when done, and the items that wait on one
    unlock.
  - The air war's and the salvage projects are ported (`lw-hangar.md`,
    second report).
  - The soldiers' projects are not: better ballistics, pistols, SCOPE,
    medikit, ammo conservation, tactical rigging and the rest. Each needs
    a tactical hook.
- **Armours' Fly** (Archangel, Seraph), **Ghost** (Shadow), air evasion
  and the psi property; the MEC suits and SHIV decks.
- The council's Future Combat bonus (a cheaper Foundry), with the
  council's bonuses.

## Report (2026-11-01, unlimited weapons fixed)

**Found while reading Long War's stores for the small items:** Long War
holds every ballistic weapon without limit (`XGStorage.Init`,
`m_arrInfiniteItems`), not only the starting kit. Phase 1 made only the
kit unlimited, so the carbine, SMG, marksman's rifle, machine pistol,
battle rifle and LMG could be neither built nor carried. Fixed:
- unlimited is now Long War's list;
- the unlimited items are put in the stores of a campaign that lacks them
  whenever soldiers are armed (`LWX2Gear.EnsureInfinite`), since XCOM 2
  adds its starting items only when a campaign begins.

Checked: a new campaign's stores hold all six.

## Report (2026-11-01, phase 5: small items and the rocket launcher)

**Long War's small items are XCOM 2 utility items** (`X2Item_LWSmallItems`,
`X2Ability_LWSmallItems`, `X2Effect_LWSmallItem`; the table in
`scripts/lw-gear.mjs` `SMALL`, generated into `XComLWGear.ini`
`+SmallItems`). Each takes an XCOM 2 item's abilities, effects and look,
with Long War's numbers from its `Weapons=` line, its units converted
(metres at 1.5 a tile, radii at 96 units a tile):
- **Grenades:** HE (3 damage, radius 3), AP (4, armour ignored), Alien
  (5), Flashbang, Smoke, Chem; plus the Mimic Beacon and the Battle
  Scanner.
- **The medikit**, one charge an item (`XGUnit.UpdateItemCharges`).
- **The Rocketeer's rocket launcher**, its secondary weapon and starting
  kit (`GetInfiniteSecondary`): XCOM 2's rocket launcher with Long War's
  7 damage and radius 4. The Rocket item adds a rocket.
- **Passive items**, a stat ability each:
  - Laser Sight: +4 aim and +4 crit;
  - Ceramic Plating: +1 HP;
  - Hi-Cap and Drum Mags: a shot costs half, a third of a round
    (`XGAbility_Targeted`): every second shot, or two in three, gets its
    round back; Drum Mags also −4 aim;
  - Chameleon Suit: reaction shots never crit its wearer.
- **Which classes:** Long War's properties say who may carry each. The
  battle scanner goes to Scouts (and Engineers), Drum Mags to the rifle
  classes, the Chameleon Suit to Scouts, rockets to Rocketeers. A soldier
  carrying what their class may not has it taken back when armed.
- **Unlimited** are those Long War holds without limit. Engineering
  builds the Chem Grenade, the Mimic Beacon and the Chameleon Suit.
- **XCOM 2's one-grenade, one-medikit rule is lifted** (Long War stacks
  them, a charge each). XCOM 2's frag grenade on a Long War soldier
  becomes Long War's HE grenade.

**Checked in a mission:**
- the Sniper given a second HE grenade threw with two charges;
- the Rocketeer's launcher had two rockets with the Rocket item;
- HP included Tac Armor.

New control port test aids:
- `give-to <soldier> <item>`;
- `abilities` (each soldier's usable abilities and charges, in a
  mission).

### Decisions

- **Grenades and rockets do fixed damage** (spread 0), as Enemy
  Within's explosives do.
- **The Laser Sight's crit counts for every weapon.** Long War gives it
  only to non-pistols.
- **Class limits on small items are enforced when soldiers are armed.**
  XCOM 2's armory has no per-class limit for utility items.

### Left open

- The perks that change charges or add items (Grenadier, Packmaster,
  Smoke Grenade, Battle Scanner, Fire Rocket's second rocket and the
  rest), and the Shredder Rocket.
- The arc thrower (next), the Shadow Device, SCOPE, the Battle Computer
  and the other built items.

## Report (2026-11-01, the arc thrower; invisible bodies fixed)

**The arc thrower takes aliens alive in played missions** (`X2Ability_LWArcThrower`,
`X2AbilityToHitCalc_LWStun`, `X2Condition_LWStunnable`, `X2Effect_LWCapture`). It
is a small item, built in engineering once the Arc Thrower is researched (§100,
10 days, 20 engineers), with one charge an item.
- **The shot** targets a visible alien within 4 tiles and ends the soldier's
  turn. It never targets robots, chryssalids, zombies (Long War's
  `CanBeStunned`), the Lost, the Chosen or the Avatar.
- **Its chance** is Enemy Within's stun chance × Long War's 0.85
  (`UFO_PSI_LINK_SURVIVE`, `TryStunned`). Enemy Within's own part is in its
  native code and can't be read, so it is an APPROXIMATION: 100 − 50 × the
  alien's health share. A full-health alien is 42 %.
- **A stun takes the alien off the field** and notes it in the Geoscape's
  state, which XCOM 2 brings back from the mission.
  - When the mission is won, it comes home as Long War's captive of that
    kind, to Alien Containment with its plasma weapon, or dead without
    containment.
  - XCOM 2's kinds stand in for Long War's species until Enemy Within's
    aliens are the missions' (`+CaptureAs` in `XComLWStrategy.ini`,
    APPROXIMATION): Sectoid as Sectoid, Viper and ADVENT as Thin Man, Muton,
    Archon as Heavy Floater, Berserker, Andromedon as Muton Elite, Codex as
    Sectoid Commander.
- **Checked in a mission:**
  - the stun was offered on the Sniper's arc thrower (one charge), and its
    roll logged "42 % → miss" for a full-health Sectoid;
  - the capture applied to a Sectoid took it off the field, and the won
    mission brought home "Taken alive: Sectoid Captive to Alien Containment,
    Alien Pistol".
  - Not checked: a stun from start to finish on a visible alien. Units
    dropped by XCOM 2's cheat never became visible in the missions tried, so
    even a standard shot couldn't target them; the roll and the capture were
    checked separately.

**Invisible torsos and arms, which the person saw, are fixed.** XCOM 2's
base-game torsos, arms and legs share names across part types. The armours'
clones of them were named objects, so a torso's clone was overwritten by the
legs' and loaded a legs mesh. They are now made unnamed, as XCOM 2 makes its
own. Checked in the armory: a soldier in the base-game torso and arms, whole.

New control port aids:
- `parts`, each soldier's body parts;
- `armory <n>`, XCOM 2's armory on a soldier;
- in missions, `enemies`, `spawn`, `capture` and `use <soldier> <ability>`;
- `tools/test/to-mission.sh`, a fresh campaign into a mission.
