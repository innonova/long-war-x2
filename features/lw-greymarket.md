## Purpose

Long War's grey market buys corpses, artifacts, resources and gear for cash, which matters
for its tight economy. The port had none. The person (2026-11-15): the strategy layer moves
mostly to EW's own screens.

## Report (2026-11-15)

- **Prices** come from stock Long War (`XGItemTree.GetItemSalePrice`, `CanBeSold`),
  generated by `scripts/gear-config.mjs` into `[LongWarX2.LWGreyMarket]`:
  - each item's `ItemBalance_Normal` cash, 40% of it for an item engineering builds;
  - never craft, their weapons, the satellite or the Skyranger;
  - corpses marked, for Xenological Remedies;
  - alloys and elerium are XCOM 2's own resources in the port.
- **`LWGreyMarket`:**
  - what XCOM holds that can be sold (nothing it holds without limit);
  - the price now (Xenological Remedies raises corpses');
  - the alien grenade only until the Foundry makes them;
  - NOT RESEARCHED for an item whose research isn't done (EW's
    `XGGreyMarketUI.BuildItemProperties`);
  - the sale: items taken, cash paid, an event noted.
- **`UILWEWGreyMarket`** is EW's GreyMarket movie, driven as `UIGreyMarket` drives it:
  - each item with how many are in storage, the price, how many to sell (arrows on the
    selected row, or left and right keys) and what they bring;
  - the total;
  - the selected item's words and picture.

  COMPLETE TRANSACTION sells them. EW puts that button on its HUD's help bar; here it's an
  XCOM 2 button, which renders inside EW's movie. Enter does the same.
- The Geoscape has a GREY MARKET button when EW's screens are converted. Control port:
  `open greymarket`.

Seen on wingpu (China, Xenological Remedies): Sectoid Corpse at §4 (Long War's §2,
doubled), Weapon Fragment §1, Meld §4. Three corpses marked for sale and sold took money
from §500 to §512.

### Decisions

- The movie hides a row's arrows after each click and doesn't report hover. So the
  selected row's arrows are shown again a moment after each change (`ShowArrows`).
  Hovering a row doesn't select it. A click on its arrows or the up and down keys do.
