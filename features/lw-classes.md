## Purpose

The first step of the port (`port-plan.md`): Long War 1.0's soldiers in
XCOM 2. A promoted soldier shows a Long War class (Sniper, Scout,
Rocketeer, Gunner, Medic, Engineer, Assault, Infantry) with Long War's perk
tree in XCOM 2's promotion screen: three choices per rank, from Corporal to
Colonel, with Long War's stat bonuses. The draw is XCOM 2's soldiers; this
gives them Long War's careers.

## Requirements

- The eight subclasses as XCOM 2 soldier classes, their trees taken from
  Long War's own `DefaultGameCore.ini` (`[XComGame.XComPerkManager]
  SoldierPerkTrees`, types 11–14 and 21–24, stat bonuses 15–18 and 25–28),
  generated by a script, never copied by hand.
- Each Long War perk as an XCOM 2 ability: XCOM 2's own where it is the
  same perk (Squadsight, Snap Shot, Run and Gun, Lightning Reflexes …),
  else a named stand-in, listed, to be built in later rounds.
- Long War's rank names and its seven ranks.
- Not: Long War's random class assignment and MEC/psi careers yet; not the
  perks' full mechanics.

## Hard to verify

The promotion screen with three columns per rank (XCOM 2's shows two plus
the XCOM row): seen in the game, with a screenshot.

## Facts

- Long War's trees: `../xcom-ew-lw-tree/samples/Config/DefaultGameCore.ini`
  (the person's install; lines about 3114–3220), with the "nonobvious
  perks" table above them (Long War renamed EW perk slots: Sharpshooter is
  `ePerk_Medal_UrbanB` …).
- XCOM 2's classes: `XComClassData.ini` and `X2SoldierClassTemplate` in the
  SDK.
