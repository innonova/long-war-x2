## Purpose

The first step of the port (`port-plan.md`): Long War 1.0's soldiers in
XCOM 2. A promoted soldier shows a Long War class (Sniper, Scout,
Rocketeer, Gunner, Medic, Engineer, Assault, Infantry) with Long War's perk
tree in XCOM 2's promotion screen: three choices per rank, from Corporal to
Colonel, with Long War's stat bonuses. The draw is XCOM 2's soldiers; this
gives them Long War's careers.

## Requirements

- The eight subclasses as XCOM 2 soldier classes, their trees taken from
  Long War's own `DefaultGameCore.ini` (`[XComGame.XComPerkManager]
  SoldierPerkTrees`, types 11–14 and 21–24, stat bonuses 15–18 and 25–28),
  generated by a script, never copied by hand.
- Each Long War perk as an XCOM 2 ability: XCOM 2's own where it is the
  same perk (Squadsight, Snap Shot, Run and Gun, Lightning Reflexes …),
  else a named stand-in, listed, to be built in later rounds.
- Long War's rank names and its seven ranks.
- Not: Long War's random class assignment and MEC/psi careers yet; not the
  perks' full mechanics.

## Hard to verify

The promotion screen with three columns per rank (XCOM 2's shows two plus
the XCOM row): seen in the game, with a screenshot.

## Facts

- Long War's trees: `../xcom-ew-lw-tree/samples/Config/DefaultGameCore.ini`
  (the person's install; lines about 3114–3220), with the "nonobvious
  perks" table above them (Long War renamed EW perk slots: Sharpshooter is
  `ePerk_Medal_UrbanB` …).
- XCOM 2's classes: `XComClassData.ini` and `X2SoldierClassTemplate` in the
  SDK.

## Report (2026-11-01 night, the classes in the game) — the structure stands

**Seen in the game** (a test campaign; screenshots in the conversation): the
barracks list Long War's classes (Sniper, Scout, Rocketeer, Gunner, Medic,
Engineer, Assault, Infantry); a Corporal Sniper carries a sniper rifle and
a pistol and has Squadsight; Promote Soldier opens the heroes' screen with
Long War's tree: Squaddie (Squadsight, the pistol shot), Corporal's three
choices with their own icons and Long War's words ("Deadeye: Gain +15 aim
against flying targets"), cost 0.

**What there is**
- `scripts/classes-config.mjs` writes, from the player's Long War install:
  `Config/XComClassData.ini` (the eight classes, their trees, their stat
  gains under Long War's normal settings, their weapons and squaddie
  loadouts; XCOM 2's four classes taken out of the recruits' deck),
  `Config/XComLWClasses.ini` (the stand-ins) and
  `Localization/XComGame.int` (the words; not in the repository).
- Nine perks are XCOM 2's own abilities (Squadsight, Lightning Reflexes,
  Run & Gun, In The Zone, Covering Fire, Sentinel, Rapid Fire, Holo-Targeting,
  Field Medic); 57 are stand-ins (`X2Ability_LWStandins`: a passive with
  the name, words and icon, 44 with an icon of their own from XCOM 2's
  perk icons, which still carry many of EW's).
- `UIScreenListener_LongWarX2Promotion` opens the heroes' screen for Long
  War's classes.
- For development, `PromoteRookies` makes the rookies Corporals of the
  eight classes on the Avenger.

### Decisions

- **The heroes' promotion screen** for three choices a rank: XCOM 2's normal
  screen has two columns built into its movie; the heroes' screen takes four.
  It hides the ranks not reached yet ("?"), where Long War shows the whole
  tree; to change in a later round.
- **XCOM 2's ability where the perk is the same**, with XCOM 2's numbers
  for now; Long War's numbers when the abilities are written.
- **Stand-ins rather than leaving gaps**: the trees are whole and readable
  today; each stand-in is replaced by a real ability in its own round.
- **Long War's words generated on the player's machine**, not committed:
  they are Long War's text. The class config (structure, numbers, names of
  abilities) is in the repository.
- **Weapons by XCOM 2's categories**: Sniper and Scout sniper rifles (Scout
  also rifles), Gunner cannons, Assault shotguns and rifles, the others
  rifles, everyone a pistol. Rocketeers have no rocket launcher yet (XCOM
  2's is a heavy weapon on armour).

**Left**: the 57 abilities; one choice a rank (the heroes' screen lets a
soldier buy more with ability points); the whole tree visible; Long War's
rank names; its per-perk stat bonuses (tree types 15–18, 25–28); the
Rocketeer's rockets; class icons of their own.

## Report (2026-11-01 night, the first perks working)

Eleven of Long War's own perks now work, with Long War's numbers:
`X2Effect_LWModifiers` gives aim, critical chance, damage, defence and
immunity to critical hits, each under a condition (no ally within N tiles,
target below N% health, per enemy in sight up to a cap, height advantage, no
action taken yet, target in full cover, in low cover, the shot's weapon);
`X2Ability_LWStandins` builds a passive with that effect (and a mobility
bonus) for each perk whose definition the generator gives. The numbers are
EW's constants (`XGTacticalGameCoreNativeBase`, read with upkit from the
player's `XComGame.upk`) and Long War's ini: Lone Wolf +10/+10 beyond 7
tiles, Sharpshooter, Executioner +10/+10 at or below 50%, Aggression +10 per
enemy (max 3), Tactical Sense +5 per enemy (max 4), Damn Good Ground +10/+10,
Platform Stability +10/+10, Sprinter +4 mobility, Resilience, Low Profile,
Ranger +1 damage.

The same numbers now fill Long War's words (EW fills `<XGAbility:…/>` tags
at run time; three tags have no value found yet).

**Seen**: a Gunner Colonel made on the quick launch's squad screen (class,
rank, a cannon, abilities ticked from Long War's tree) goes into a mission
on the EW map with Ranger, Resilience, Platform Stability and Executioner in
play (logged from the unit's effects). Not yet seen: the numbers in a shot's
breakdown (no enemy in sight in that run).

**Found**: a mod's class config must add to arrays with `+`
(`+AllowedWeapons`): plain lines replaced each other and the classes could
carry only a pistol.

### Decisions

- **One effect class with named conditions** for the bonus perks, the
  conditions chosen by the generator: most of Long War's passives are a
  number under a condition, so each becomes a line of config.
- **XCOM 2's cover values for Low Profile** (low cover counted as full:
  +20 defence), since XCOM 2's cover numbers replace EW's.

## Report (2026-11-01 night, five more perks; not yet seen in a fight)

Sixteen of Long War's own perks now have effects (plus nine as XCOM 2's
own abilities). New tonight, built and compiled, **not yet seen in a shot**
(that needs an enemy in sight; the runs so far had none):
- **Hit and Run, Close Encounters, Light 'Em Up**: `X2Effect_LWFreeShot`
  gives a standard shot's action points back after XCOM 2 has taken them,
  once a turn, when the condition holds (an exposed or flanked target; a
  target within 6 tiles; the turn's first action, where the shot then costs
  one action instead of ending the turn).
- **HEAT Ammo**: +50% damage against robotic targets.
- **Vital Point Targeting**: +2 damage with primary weapons (Long War's
  autopsy condition and the sidearms' +1 not yet).

**Icons**: where Long War reused an EW perk's slot under a new name (Hit
and Run is EW's Reinforced Armor slot), the slot's icon belonged to another
perk; those perks now take the standard icon unless XCOM 2 has one by Long
War's name.

**Found**: six of Long War's renamed perks have no words in its
`XComGame.int` under their slot (Lock N' Load, Smoke and Mirrors, HEAT
Warheads, Will to Survive, Javelin Rockets, Sapper): Long War keeps their
text elsewhere. To find.

### Decisions

- **Free shots by refunding the cost**, the way XCOM 2's own Serial does,
  rather than new shot abilities: the soldier keeps XCOM 2's standard shot,
  its animations and its targeting.

**Seen too**: XCOM 2's "soldier promoted" popup names the rank's first
ability, not the one chosen; and the Long War headquarters opens on top of
the after-action screens (the facility grid starts underneath them). Both
to fix.

## Report (2026-11-01, Skirmish Mode and the perk probe)

**Skirmish Mode** (War of the Chosen's Legacy Hub > Skirmish) works with
Long War's classes: they are in its class list by name, a soldier can be
given any rank, and its abilities screen lists Long War's tree with Long
War's words, one perk a rank picked. It arms a soldier from weapon tables
built in code; Long War's classes are now added to them
(`LWX2SkirmishWeapons`, each armed like an XCOM 2 class: Sniper and Scout
like the Sharpshooter, Gunner like the Grenadier, Assault like the Ranger,
the rest like the Specialist; everyone a pistol). **This is the quick way
for the person to see the soldiers in a fight**, without a campaign.
(Long War's In The Zone shows XCOM 2's name for the ability it maps to,
"Serial".)

**The perk probe** (`LWX2PerkProbe`, development switch `ProbePerks`): in
any mission, each Long War soldier's perks with their numbers and whether
each condition holds against the nearest enemy, and the real shot
breakdown against the two nearest enemies, logged when they change.
`tools/test/skirmish.sh <classes>` runs a Skirmish Mode mission with the
given classes and prints it. First run: a Sniper's Sharpshooter adds +10
aim and +10 crit in the game's own breakdown; Lone Wolf, Damn Good Ground,
Aggression, Executioner and Platform Stability listed with their conditions
not holding at the start of the mission, as expected.

### Decisions

- **War of the Chosen's Skirmish Mode instead of a skirmish of our own**:
  it already does everything asked (squad, ranks, perks, map, enemies);
  only the weapon tables needed Long War's classes.

## Report (2026-11-01, the item perks)

With Long War's small items in (`lw-gear.md`, phase 5), the perks that change
their charges work (`X2Effect_LWCharges`, carried by every Long War armour's
stat ability, set at a mission's start from Long War's `XGUnit.UpdateItemCharges`):
- **Grenadier** (LW_Grenadier): HE, Alien and AP grenades ×2.
- **Smoke and Mirrors** (LW_SmokeandMirrors): flashbangs, chem grenades and
  mimic beacons ×2; +1 smoke grenade and battle scanner an item.
- **Packmaster** (LW_Packmaster, Long War's Deep Pockets): +1 charge an item
  for grenades, beacons, the arc thrower, smoke, the scanner and the medikit.
- **Repair** (LW_Repair): +2 arc thrower charges an item.
- **Smoke Bomb** (LW_SmokeGrenade) and **Battle Scanner** (LW_BattleScanner):
  +1 smoke, +2 scans.
- **Shock and Awe** (LW_ShockAndAwe, Long War's Rocketeer perk): a second
  rocket.
- **Fire Rocket**, the Rocketeer's squaddie perk, is its launcher in the kit.

**Checked in a mission** (the new test aid `perk <soldier> <ability>`):
- two HE grenades with Grenadier and Packmaster threw with 6 charges;
- a flashbang with Smoke and Mirrors had 2;
- the Rocketeer's launcher with Shock and Awe had 2 rockets.

### Decisions

- **Smoke Bomb and Battle Scanner add to a carried item.** Long War gives
  the soldier the smoke or scanner without one, outside the small slots.
  Here a soldier with the perk needs the item (APPROXIMATION).

## Report (2026-11-01 night, more perks: Precision Shot, Disabling Shot, Paramedic, Savior, Deadeye)

Long War's numbers come from EW's code constants (read from the player's
`XComGame.upk`, as the words already were) and from its ability table
(`XGAbilityTree.BuildAbilities`):

- **Precision Shot**: a shot beside the standard one, with +30 critical
  chance (`PRECISION_SHOT_CRITICAL_CHANCE_BONUS`) and +2 damage on a
  critical hit (`PRECISION_SHOT_DAMAGE_BONUS`). The bonus is a second
  damage effect that has only a critical part. Cooldown 2.
- **Disabling Shot**: a shot at −10 aim (`DISABLINGSHOT_AIM_PENALTY`)
  that cannot crit and empties the target's weapon. A reload fixes it,
  exactly as Long War's text says; XCOM 2's own `X2Effect_DisableWeapon`
  does this. Cooldown 2.
- **Both shots with a sniper rifle** (`lw_sniper`) need the whole turn,
  as the rifle's own shot does, unless the soldier has Snap Shot
  (`X2Condition_LWSniperReady`).
- **Paramedic**: one medikit use a turn costs no action (the free-action
  effect, condition `Medikit`: heal, nano heal, stabilize).
- **Savior**: medikits heal +4 (`MEDIKIT_SAVIOR_BONUS`; an extra heal
  effect on XCOM 2's medikit abilities, applied only when the medic has the
  perk), and +2 medikit charges.
- **Deadeye**: +15 aim against flying targets; in XCOM 2, units whose
  template can fly.
- The words' missing numbers are filled: Headshot crit bonus 30, both
  cooldowns 2.

**Checked in the game** (perks given by the test aid, which now binds shots
to the primary weapon):
- Both shots are on the soldiers' bars with their weapons. Fired from
  8 and 9 tiles at a Sectoid, both were used and both then reported
  `AA_CoolingDown`. The sniper condition passed with a full turn.
- **Both missed**, so the hit effects (the weapon emptied, the crit
  damage) are not seen in play yet.
- The medic with Savior and one medikit had 3 charges (1 + 2).
- **Paramedic and Savior, checked later that night.** Two frag grenades
  thrown at the squad's feet left soldiers at 1 to 2 hit points. A medic
  with both perks healed two of them, each from 2 to 8 (+6, capped at
  their maximum; XCOM 2's medikit alone heals 4).
  - After the first heal the medic still had both actions (Paramedic).
    After the second, still one.
  - The medikit's charges went from 3 to 1.

**Test aids:**
- `near <soldier> [tiles]`: puts a soldier at a floor tile that many tiles
  from the nearest alien.
- Aliens dropped by `spawn` never become visible, on XCOM 2's maps as well
  as EW's (their tile is not floor, and they get no visibility update).
  `near` is the way to get a target in view.

### Decisions

- **Cooldown 2 for both shots.** EW's ability table gives them 5,
  counted, as EW counts cooldowns, on every side's turn. That is read as 2
  of the soldier's own turns (APPROXIMATION).
- **Precision Shot's crit damage is +2 for every weapon.** Long War's
  grows with the weapon's tech; only the base constant is known.
- **"Ignores long-range critical penalties"** has nothing to act on:
  XCOM 2 has no such penalty.
- **Savior's two charges are on the medikit the soldier carries.** Long
  War's are on no item; XCOM 2 keeps charges on items.

### Left open (perks)

- Still stand-ins: Flush, Double Tap, Rapid Reaction, Ready for Anything,

## Report (2026-11-01 night, Dense Smoke and Combat Drugs)

**Dense Smoke** (+20 defence, `DENSE_SMOKE_DEFENSE_BONUS`) and **Combat
Drugs** (+20 will, `COMBAT_DRUGS_WILL_BONUS`; +10 critical chance,
`COMBAT_DRUGS_CRITICAL_CHANCE_BONUS`) work in the cloud of Long War's smoke
grenade.
- `X2Effect_LWSmoke` is added to the grenade's thrown and launched
  effects, and applies only when the thrower has the perk.
- Like XCOM 2's smoke effect, it counts while the unit stands in smoke and
  lasts as long as the smoke.

**Checked in the game:** a smoke grenade thrown at a soldier's feet by a
soldier with both perks.
- The two soldiers in the cloud carried XCOM 2's `SmokeGrenade` effect and
  `LW_DenseSmoke_Smoke` and `LW_CombatDrugs_Smoke`, with will 70 and 67
  (+20).
- The two outside the cloud had none of them.
- The defence and crit modifiers themselves were not read from a shot's
  breakdown.

**Test aids:**
- `use <soldier> <ability>:<item> 98`: an item's ability (each grenade has
  its own ThrowGrenade), aimed at the soldier's own tile, with the units in
  its area gathered as XCOM 2's targeting does. Without that gathering only
  the world smoke appeared.
- `effects`: each soldier's will, hit points, whether they stand in
  smoke, and the effects on them.

### Decisions

- **Combat Drugs' crit is +10, its constant.** Long War's text says +20.
- **The will is given for the effect's whole time**, and the stat change
  stays until the effect ends. A stat change has no check for standing in
  smoke; the defence and crit parts do have one.
- **Dense Smoke's wider cloud (×1.25) is not done.**

## Report (2026-11-02 night, Double Tap, Rapid Reaction, Ready for Anything)

These three go through the free-action effect (`X2Effect_LWFreeShot`),
which now also gives reserve points back or adds them:
- **Double Tap**: the turn's first shot leaves the second action (standard,
  sniper, snap, Precision and Disabling shots). After that, 3 turns before
  it works again (`DOUBLETAP_COOLDOWN`, counted on the player's turn
  count).
- **Rapid Reaction**: a reaction shot (overwatch, pistol overwatch, Long
  Watch) that hits gives its reserve point back, twice a turn at most, so
  three shots in all, as Long War's text says.
- **Ready for Anything**: a shot taken as the turn's first action, which
  ends the turn, leaves the soldier in overwatch (a reserve point added).

**Checked in the game** (perks given by the test aid; soldiers put 8 tiles
from a pod; the `effects` aid now shows action and reserve points):
- Double Tap: after the first shot the soldier had 1 action and fired
  again, then had 0.
- Ready for Anything: after the shot the soldier had 0 actions and an
  `overwatch` reserve point.
- **Rapid Reaction is not tried in play**: it needs an alien to walk into
  the overwatch.

### Decisions

- **Ready for Anything adds the reserve point when the shot is taken**, not
  at the end of the turn. The shot ends the turn, so the effect is the
  same, but XCOM 2's overwatch eye is not shown on the unit.
- **Rapid Reaction's minimum distance (`RAPIDREACTION_MIN_DIST`, 2 tiles)
  is not applied.**

## Report (2026-11-02 night, Close Combat Specialist and Shredder Ammo)

- **Close Combat Specialist**: a reaction shot with the weapon at an enemy
  who moves or acts within 4 tiles, without overwatch; once a turn for each
  enemy, hit or miss. It is built as XCOM 2's Bladestorm is (triggered on
  movement and on attacks, the target marked for the turn), with a shot.
- **Shredder Ammo**: a unit hit by the Gunner's LMG or SAW (`lw_gunner`)
  or by a rocket from a soldier with the perk takes 33 % more damage from
  every source for 4 turns (`SHREDDER_ROCKET_DURATION`).
  `X2Effect_LWShredded` is added to the standard shot, the overwatch shot
  and the rocket launcher, under two conditions: the shooter has the perk,
  and the weapon is one of those (`X2Condition_LWSourceWeapon`).

**Checked in the game:**
- Close Combat Specialist: soldiers put 3 and 4 tiles from a pod of
  ADVENT MECs, the turn ended. The pod moved and was shot. The Gunner's
  ammo went from 6 to 1, the Scout's from 3 to 2, and the MECs were left
  at 3/7, 4/7 and 5/7.
- Shredder Ammo: the Gunner's hit left a Sectoid at 2/8, carrying
  `LWShredded`. The extra damage of a later hit was not measured.
- The `enemies` aid now lists each alien's effects.

### Decisions

- **+33 % for Shredder.** EW applies the shred in native code (its script
  only counts the turns down: `m_iShredderRocketCtr`, at most 4), so the
  factor cannot be read. 33 % is EW's documented one (APPROXIMATION).
- **Close Combat Specialist fires at every enemy that closes in**, once each
  a turn, not once a turn in all.

## Report (2026-11-02 night, Sapper and HEAT Warheads)

- **Sapper**: grenades do +1 damage (condition `Grenade`: grenades only,

- **HEAT Warheads**: rockets and grenades do +50 % to robots

- **Checked:** a frag grenade (base 3) thrown by a soldier with Sapper took
  4 from a Sectoid. HEAT Warheads is not seen in play: no robot was in the

- **Test aid:** `use <soldier> <ability>:<item> 97` aims at the nearest

- **Not done, for want of Long War's numbers (they are in EW's code, not

- **Not done, for want of a mechanism:** Lock N' Load's extra shot (XCOM 2
  changes a clip's size only through weapon upgrades; its reload already
  leaves the turn going).

## Report (2026-11-02 night, Flush)

- **Flush**: a shot beside the standard one, at +30 aim
  (`FLUSH_AIM_BONUS`), half damage (`FLUSH_DMG_REDUCTION`) and two shots'
  ammo. The halving is a hidden passive that comes with it
  (`LW_Flush_Damage`, condition `FlushShot`).
- **Checked:** from 6 tiles, Flush hit an ADVENT MEC for 1 (7 → 6) and the
